# Staff guide (one page)

**Install:** open https://team.biglitz.in on your phone, then "Add to Home screen".

**First login:** enter your mobile number and the temporary 4-digit PIN your Admin gave you. You must then choose your own PIN (not 1234, 0000, repeated digits, or your mobile's last 4).

**Every day**
1. **Punch in** (Home): allow Location and Camera. Stand within the shop radius, take a live selfie. If you are too far, the app shows how many metres away you are.
2. During the day: open **Tasks** and tap *Done* as you finish. Open **Leads**, call or WhatsApp from the lead, and update the status after each contact.
3. If your unit has sales: enter today's sales (Home → Sales today). Type 0 for categories with no sales, or tap *No sales today*. You cannot punch out until this is done. You can edit it until midnight.
4. **Punch out** (Home): take the selfie. The app shows "Today you earned" (salary + estimated incentive).

**Leave:** More → Leave. Apply at least 2 days ahead. Less than 2 days ahead is a *late application*: even if approved, it counts as absent for your Discipline Score. 2 leave days per month are free; extra days reduce salary.

**Colours on your attendance calendar:** green on time, yellow late, blue half day, orange missing punch-out, violet leave, red absent.

**Discipline Score** = average of attendance % and on-time %. Be on time (punch in by shift start, punch out at or after shift end) to build a streak and badges. Top 3 of the month win ₹5,000 / ₹3,000 / ₹1,000.

**Payslips:** More → Payslips (available after payroll is approved).

**Forgot PIN / locked out (5 wrong tries = 15 minutes):** ask your Admin to reset it.
