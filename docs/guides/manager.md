# Manager guide (one page)

You have everything Staff have, plus the **Team** tab. You can see only your own team.

**Team → Today:** who is present, late, absent, on leave, and whose sales are not in yet. Tap any number to see the list; tap a person to open them.

**Team → Tasks:** each person's tasks. *No task right now* shows people who are punched in with nothing open: give them work. Tap a person → Tasks → *Add task* (set a due time, priority, or repeat: daily, weekly, monthly, month end). You can reopen a task that was marked done.

**Team → Leads:** follow-ups due, overdue, won and lost for the team. Open a lead to reassign it. **Pools:** set the order people receive new leads (round robin). Tick "Skip absent" so absent or on-leave people are skipped.

**Team → Sales:** today and month by category and by person.

**Team → Leave:** approve or reject. A *late application* (under 2 days notice) still counts as absent for discipline even if you approve it. You cannot approve your own leave.

**Corrections** (person → Attendance → tap a day): set punch times, mark half day, or mark absent. A reason is always required and is recorded. You can also correct a sales entry from the Sales tab. After payroll is approved for a month, nothing in that month can be changed.

**Targets:** person → Target. Set the monthly target and the incentive slabs (default: below 80% nothing, 80-99% 1%, 100%+ 1.5% of the whole month's sales). Add a reason.

**Ranking:** Discipline ranking of your unit. You never see anyone's salary or documents.
